    ParcelCare Help Guide

    1. Add a parcel with the "Add Parcel" button.
    2. Find it by typing its ID in "Find Your Parcel", or click it in My Parcels.
    3. Record progress with "Add a tracking checkpoint" (demo data entered by hand).
    4. Reschedule if you will not be home.
    5. Report damage with a photo as proof.
    6. Not received? Use Delivery Verification on a parcel marked Delivered.
    7. Cancel a parcel and follow the refund steps.
